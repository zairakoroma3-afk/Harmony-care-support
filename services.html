SERVICES WE PROVIDE

Detailed Non-Medical Home Support Services

Our non-medical home support services focus on personal care, routine household tasks, companionship, escort support, and oversight rather than clinical nursing interventions.

SERVICE OVERVIEW

Support That Helps Clients Manage Daily Life at Home

Below is a detailed overview of the non-medical services Harmony Care Support LLC provides.

01

Personal Care Services (Activities of Daily Living - ADLs)

Direct, hands-on physical assistance provided to clients in their homes.

  • Bathing & Hygiene: Assistance with tub baths, showers, bed baths, hair washing, skin care, oral hygiene, and grooming.
  • Dressing & Undressing: Assistance with selecting clothes, putting on garments, adaptive clothing, and footwear.
  • Toileting & Incontinence Maintenance: Help with bathroom use, commode transfers, changing protective garments, and maintaining peri-care hygiene.
  • Mobility, Transfers & Positioning: Assisting with bed-to-chair or wheelchair transfers, ambulation support inside the home, and routine positioning changes.
  • Eating & Feeding Support: Non-clinical assistance with intake during mealtime.
02

Household Support & Chore Services (Instrumental ADLs - IADLs)

Services designed to maintain a clean, safe, and functional home environment.

  • Meal Preparation & Planning: Grocery shopping, preparing routine daily meals according to client dietary needs, and post-meal clean-up.
  • Light Housekeeping: Cleaning client-occupied areas including dusting, sweeping, vacuuming, mopping, taking out trash, cleaning bathroom surfaces, and bed-making.
  • Laundry & Linen Management: Washing, drying, folding, ironing, and putting away personal clothes and bed linens.
  • Essential Errands & Shopping: Picking up non-prescription medications, running local household errands, and acquiring daily essential supplies.
03

Escort, Transportation & Companion Support

Non-clinical support aimed at reducing isolation and helping clients manage routine appointments.

  • Appointment Escort: Accompaniment and transportation to medical appointments, social service agencies, or administrative errands.
  • Community Engagement: Accompanying individuals on neighborhood walks, local park visits, or social activities.
  • In-Home Companionship: Structured social engagement, cognitive exercises, reading, conversation, and recreational activities.
04

Oversight & Routine Monitoring

Practical day-to-day observation and reminders that support client safety and service consistency.

  • Medication Prompts & Reminders: Offering verbal reminders to take self-administered medications on schedule. Direct administration requires TME certification or RN delegation.
  • Safety Observation: Monitoring client stability, preventing fall hazards, and reporting changes in condition or environment to the supervising RN.
  • Service Plan Documentation: Maintaining daily task logs and tracking service delivery according to the individual client service plan.
PERSONALIZED SUPPORT

Services Planned Around the Person

We understand that clients may need different kinds of help depending on their routines, physical abilities, goals, and household situation. Our services are designed to be practical, respectful, and helpful in real everyday life.

Whether a client needs more assistance with personal care, companionship, household tasks, appointment support, or routine reminders, our goal is to provide service that brings comfort, consistency, and peace of mind.

Common Reasons Families Reach Out

  • Support with daily activities at home
  • Extra help maintaining a safe routine
  • Companionship and supervision for a loved one
  • Help balancing care responsibilities within the family
  • Assistance after a change in independence or function
