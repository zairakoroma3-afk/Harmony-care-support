Meet Hawanatu Dumbuya

CEO / Executive Director of Harmony Care Support LLC

ABOUT OUR CEO

A Leader Focused on Compassion, Excellence, and Growth

Hawanatu Dumbuya is the CEO and Executive Director of Harmony Care Support LLC. She leads the company with a strong commitment to high standards, thoughtful service, professional growth, and person-centered support.

Hawanatu believes quality support is about more than just completing tasks. To her, quality means listening well, protecting dignity, respecting the client’s home, showing up consistently, and helping families feel confident that their loved one is in caring and capable hands.

Her vision is to build a company known not only for services provided, but also for the feeling clients and families have when working with Harmony Care Support LLC — a feeling of trust, comfort, calm, respect, and professionalism.

As CEO, she is focused on building systems that support both clients and staff, strengthening the company’s presence in the community, and creating a service experience that reflects warmth, accountability, and excellence.
